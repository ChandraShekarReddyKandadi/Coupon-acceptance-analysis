# Coupon Acceptance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes survey data to identify customer characteristics and driving situations associated with coupon acceptance.

## Key Findings

- Overall coupon acceptance was approximately 56.9%.
- Frequent bar visitors were more likely to accept Bar coupons.
- Regular coffee-house customers were more likely to accept Coffee House coupons.
- One-day Coffee House coupons performed better than two-hour coupons.

## Recommendations

- Target coupons toward customers who already visit the relevant business.
- Send Bar coupons primarily to frequent bar visitors traveling without children.
- Prefer one-day Coffee House coupons delivered around 10 AM or 2 PM.
- Use a safe, hands-free delivery method that does not distract drivers.
- Validate the findings with an A/B test before implementation.

## Limitations

The analysis identifies associations between customer characteristics and stated coupon acceptance, but it does not establish causation or actual redemption. It also does not measure driving distraction.
